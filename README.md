# 🍔 Food and Beverage Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes **Food and Beverage** sales performance using **Power BI**. It provides comprehensive insights into revenue trends, order patterns, salesperson performance, channel distribution, and product category breakdown across all quarters and months.

---

## 📈 Key Features of Dashboard
- 💰 Total Revenue (17.91M), Total Orders (53K) & ATP (340.73) — KPI Summary Cards
- 📅 Quarter & Channel & Product Category — Interactive Slicers
- 📉 Revenue and Orders by Month — combo bar & line chart
- 🏆 Revenue by Top 5 Salesperson — horizontal bar chart
- 📦 Orders by Top 5 Salesperson — bar chart
- 🛒 Revenue by Channel — donut chart (Online, Retail, Distributor)
- 🍽️ Revenue by Product Category — donut chart (Food, Drink)
- 📊 Revenue and Orders by Quarter — bar chart (Qtr 1–4)
- 🌾 Revenue by Product Group — detailed horizontal bar chart

---

## 🧠 Insights Derived
- **Total Revenue:** 17.91M | **Total Orders:** 53K | **ATP (Average Transaction Price):** 340.73
- **Carla Ferreira** is the top salesperson by revenue (~4M), followed by Julio Lima
- **Retail** channel dominates with **8.7M** revenue (vs Online 3.11M, Distributor 6.1M)
- **Food** category leads heavily at **16.37M** vs Drink at **1.54M**
- **Q3 & Q4** show the strongest quarterly revenue performance
- **November** recorded the peak monthly revenue across the year
- Product groups like **Wheat Flour, Oil, Yeasts** are among the top revenue contributors
